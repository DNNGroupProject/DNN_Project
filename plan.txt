phase 6


Now let's discuss the ablation study

Many students misunderstand this.

An ablation study answers one question:

Which component of our proposed method actually contributes to the improvement?

Suppose your final model is:

Baseline
        │
        ▼
Coordinate Attention
        │
        ▼
Light Decoder
        │
        ▼
Boundary Loss
        │
        ▼
Ghost Module

You claim

"Our architecture is better."

A reviewer immediately asks:

Which of these four changes actually made it better?

So you remove or add one component at a time and measure the effect.

This is exactly what reviewers want: proof that each design decision 
is justified by experiments, not just that the final model happens to work.
